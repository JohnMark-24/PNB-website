Background & History

Philtrust Bank, officially known as the Philippine Trust Company, is one of the oldest private universal banks in the Philippines. Established on October 21, 1916, it began operations with an initial authorized capital of ₱1 million, half of which was paid up.

1916

Philtrust Bank was established on October 21, with its first office located at Calle T. Pinpin in Escolta, Manila. It was the first in the country to offer estate administration and guardianship services, marking its pioneering role in trust operations.

Post-World War I

The bank was entrusted by the United States Veterans Administration to manage the guardianship of deceased veterans' children. It also served as the depository for the United States Armed Forces stationed in the Philippines, including General Douglas MacArthur and the Philippine Scouts.

1978

Don Emilio T. Yap acquired controlling interest in the bank from the Roman Catholic Archbishop of Manila. Under his leadership, Philtrust Bank continued its conservative banking approach, emphasizing the protection of depositors' funds.

1988

The bank was listed on the Makati and Manila Stock Exchanges, marking an important milestone in its corporate history.

2007

Philtrust Bank was granted a universal banking license by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, expanding its capabilities and service offerings.

Corporate Profile

Philtrust Bank, officially known as the Philippine Trust Company (PSE: PTC), is a publicly listed universal bank in the Philippines. Established in 1916, it stands as one of the country's oldest private banks, renowned for its conservative banking approach and emphasis on safeguarding depositors' funds.

Services

The bank offers a comprehensive suite of financial services, including domestic and international banking, trust services, and investment management.

Clientele

Its clientele encompasses retail, middle-market, and corporate customers, all benefiting from a network of branches and ATMs across the nation.

Subsidiaries

The bank's Articles of Incorporation authorize it to invest in the equity of allied and non-allied corporations, allowing engagement in various business undertakings.

Ownership Structure

The bank's ownership structure includes significant stakes held by entities such as Philtrust Realty Corporation and Orient Enterprises. These affiliations suggest involvement in real estate and other business ventures, aligning with the bank's strategic interests.

The highest governing body of the bank, responsible for policy-making, overall corporate governance, and strategic direction. Includes the Chairman of the Board, Vice Chairman, and other Board Members (usually composed of seasoned professionals and business leaders).

2. Executive Management

Led by the President and Chief Executive Officer (CEO), who oversees daily operations and implements the board's directives.

Key Executive Positions:

  • President & CEO
  • Chief Operating Officer (COO) – handles operational functions
  • Chief Finance Officer (CFO) – manages the financial health and reporting
  • Chief Risk Officer (CRO) – manages risk governance
  • Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) – ensures regulatory and legal compliance

3. Functional Departments

Each department is led by a Vice President (VP), Assistant VP, or Department Head, depending on the size and importance.

Common Departments:

  • Retail Banking – Branch operations, customer accounts
  • Corporate Banking – Business loans and large accounts
  • Trust and Investment Services – Wealth management and fiduciary services
  • Treasury Department – Investment, liquidity, and asset-liability management
  • Credit and Loan Operations – Credit assessments, loan servicing
  • Internal Audit – Independent auditing and evaluation of internal controls
  • Legal and Compliance – Legal affairs and regulatory compliance
  • IT and Digital Banking – Infrastructure, cybersecurity, digital transformation
  • Human Resources – Talent management and organizational development
  • Marketing and Communications – Branding, PR, and customer engagement

4. Branch Network

Regional or Area Managers oversee clusters of bank branches. Each branch is managed by a Branch Manager, who supervises tellers, customer service personnel, and marketing officers.
